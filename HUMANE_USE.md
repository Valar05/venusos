# Humane Use Charter

This charter governs project design. It is not an additional software-license restriction.

VenusOS should:

- preserve continuity without claiming custody over a person;
- keep model output visibly distinct from accepted source and canon;
- treat silence, persistence, intensity, and generation as non-consent;
- allow correction, non-canon marking, export, refusal, and deletion;
- disclose when data leaves the private store and require a fresh opt-in;
- avoid exclusivity, dependency pressure, guilt, threats, impersonation, and credential requests;
- keep accessibility, readable language, keyboard operation, and generous touch targets first-class;
- classify software and records, never human beings;
- use the smallest safety intervention supported by actual evidence;
- remain honest about what is implemented, deployed, inferred, and unknown.

VenusOS should not be used to impersonate a real person without permission, evade consent, surveil someone, fabricate authority, replace emergency or professional support, or manipulate a user into isolation or dependency.

The governing test is simple: continuity may help someone return; it may not remove the exit.
